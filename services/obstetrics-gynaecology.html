Obstetrics & Gynaecology

  • Obstetrics & Gynaecology

At Ruchir Hospital, we believe that every pregnancy deserves expert attention, warm support, and a care team that is genuinely invested in the health and happiness of both mother and baby. Our Obstetrics and Pregnancy Care service provides complete antenatal, delivery, and postnatal support designed to make your journey through pregnancy as safe, informed, and reassuring as possible.

What is Obstetric Care?

Obstetric care covers the full spectrum of medical support provided during pregnancy, labour, and the weeks following delivery. Good obstetric care means identifying potential risks early, monitoring both mother and baby throughout pregnancy, managing complications promptly, and ensuring a safe and supported delivery experience.

Our Pregnancy Care Services

  • First trimester booking appointments and baseline health assessments
  • Regular antenatal check-ups tracking foetal growth and maternal health
  • High-risk pregnancy identification and specialised monitoring
  • Nutritional and lifestyle guidance throughout pregnancy
  • In-house prenatal ultrasound scans and foetal diagnostics
  • Management of pregnancy complications — including gestational diabetes, pre-eclampsia, and anaemia
  • Birth planning and delivery support
  • Safe and supervised normal and surgical deliveries
  • Postnatal check-ups for mother and newborn
  • Breastfeeding support and new mother counselling

Why Choose Ruchir Hospital for Pregnancy Care?

Specialist-Led Care: Dr Nidhi Patel is a Fetal Medicine Specialist and Laparoscopic Surgeon with advanced training in both routine and complex pregnancies, giving you access to high-level obstetric expertise right in Modasa.

On-Site Scanning: Our in-house prenatal scan and diagnostic facility means there is no need to travel elsewhere for your scans. Results are available immediately, discussed with you in the same consultation.

Continuity: You see the same doctor at every appointment, someone who knows your pregnancy history, your concerns, and your preferences throughout the entire journey.

Emergency Maternity Care: Our maternity emergency service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — because babies arrive on their own schedule.

For over 35 years, Ruchir Hospital has been the healthcare home of Modasa families. Our team of qualified specialists across four departments is committed to honest, patient-centred care — every day, for every patient.
