Vertigo Clinic Ruchir Hospital, Modasa

At Ruchir Hospital, our dedicated Vertigo Clinic provides focused diagnosis and treatment for patients experiencing dizziness, balance problems, and spinning sensations that disrupt everyday life. Vertigo is far more than just feeling dizzy; for many patients, it affects their ability to work, drive, or even move around their home safely. Our clinic is designed to get to the root cause quickly and restore your stability.

What is Vertigo?

Vertigo is a condition characterised by a false sense of movement, typically a spinning or tilting sensation even when you are completely still. It can last seconds or persist for days and is often accompanied by nausea, unsteady balance, and difficulty concentrating. While vertigo is not a disease in itself, it is a symptom of an underlying condition that requires proper diagnosis.

Common Causes We Investigate and Treat

  • Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo (BPPV) the most common cause, triggered by head movement
  • Inner ear infections and inflammation (Vestibular Neuritis, Labyrinthitis)
  • Meniere's Disease fluid imbalance in the inner ear
  • Cervicogenic vertigo arising from neck and cervical spine issues
  • Migraine-associated vertigo
  • Central causes assessed and referred where necessary

How We Treat Vertigo

Our approach begins with a thorough clinical assessment by both our ENT Surgeon and General Medicine physician, who work together to identify whether your vertigo originates in the ear, the brain, or elsewhere.

✓ Epley Manoeuvre and repositioning techniques for BPPV
✓ Vestibular rehabilitation exercises
✓ Medication to manage symptoms and reduce inner ear inflammation
✓ Dietary and lifestyle modifications for Meniere's Disease
✓ Specialist referral where central or neurological causes are suspected

Why Choose Ruchir Hospital's Vertigo Clinic?

Collaborative Diagnosis: Our ENT Surgeon Dr Jitendra Patel and General Medicine physician Dr Viranchi Patel assess vertigo together, ensuring nothing is missed and the root cause is accurately identified.

No Unnecessary Referrals: Most vertigo cases are fully diagnosed and treated within our facility, saving you multiple appointments across different clinics.

Patient Education: We make sure you understand your condition fully, including triggers to avoid and exercises to perform at home for faster recovery.
