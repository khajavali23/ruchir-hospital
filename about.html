Our Story

From a Single Vision to Modasa's Most Complete Healthcare Destination

Established in 1987, Ruchir Hospital has grown from a focused medical practice into one of Modasa's most trusted multi-specialty healthcare destinations. Over three and a half decades, we have built our reputation on one thing alone the trust of the families across Aravalli district who have chosen us for their care, generation after generation.

Today, our team of qualified specialists across General Medicine, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, ENT, and Dental work together under one roof each one trained at respected medical institutions across India, and each one committed to the same standard of honest, thorough, and compassionate care that Ruchir Hospital was founded on.

  • Founded in 1987, serving Modasa and Aravalli district for over 35 years
  • Specialists across General Medicine, Gynaecology, ENT and Dental
  • Doctors trained at leading medical institutions across India and abroad
  • 24-hour emergency care available every day of the year
  • A growing team committed to bringing complete healthcare closer to home

Meet Our Founder

Dr. Jitendra Patel The Vision Behind Ruchir Hospital

Dr. Jitendra Patel is a qualified ENT Surgeon holding his M.S. from B.J. Medical College, Ahmedabad. With a calm, methodical approach and a deep commitment to patient welfare, he has spent his career bringing specialist care to Modasa and the Aravalli district growing Ruchir Hospital from a single clinic into the trusted multi-specialty destination it is today.

His belief has always been simple that patients in Modasa deserve the same quality of care available in larger cities, without travelling far from home to receive it.

"Good medicine begins with listening. Every patient deserves to be heard, understood, and cared for not just treated. That has been our principle from day one."

Dr. Jitendra Patel, ENT Surgeon & Founder, Ruchir Hospital

Our Approach

Your Care, Handled With Intention

Every patient who walks into Ruchir Hospital receives the same level of attention thorough, unhurried, and built around their specific needs. This is the process that has guided us since 1987.

01

Schedule Your Visit

Call us, walk in, or WhatsApp us directly. Our team ensures your appointment is confirmed quickly and your time is never wasted.

02

Meet Your Specialist

Your doctor reviews your full medical history, listens to your concerns without rushing, and conducts a thorough clinical examination before reaching any conclusion.

03

Your Treatment Plan

No generic prescriptions. Every care plan at Ruchir Hospital is built around your specific condition, your lifestyle, and your long-term health not just the immediate symptom.

04

Continued Care

We do not disappear after your first visit. Follow-up consultations, progress monitoring, and ongoing guidance are part of how we ensure you recover fully and stay well.
