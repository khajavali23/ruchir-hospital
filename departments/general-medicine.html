Department of General Medicine

The Department of General Medicine at Ruchir Hospital stands as the cornerstone of comprehensive, patient-centred healthcare in Modasa and the wider Aravalli district. Our general medicine team believes that good health begins with a physician who listens one who understands not just your symptoms but the full picture of your lifestyle, history, and well-being. Whether you are walking in with a seasonal illness or managing a long-standing chronic condition, this department is your first and most trusted point of care.

We take pride in offering thorough, evidence-based consultations that leave no question unanswered and no symptom overlooked. Every patient is treated with dignity, and every diagnosis is made with precision.

  • Diabetes, Hypertension, and Heart Disease management
  • Fever, Cough, Infections, and Respiratory conditions
  • Tuberculosis diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment
  • Asthma, Allergies, and Lung Diseases
  • Stroke assessment, early intervention, and follow-up care
  • Thyroid Disorders and Liver Disease
  • Headaches, Abdominal Pain, and Vertigo
  • Preventive health screenings and routine wellness check-ups
  • Weight management consultations and lifestyle counselling
  • Vaccination and immunisation for all age groups

Why Choose Ruchir Hospital for General Medicine?

At Ruchir Hospital, General Medicine is not just about treating illness; it is about building a relationship of trust. Our physician takes the time to understand your full medical history, identify risk factors before they become problems, and coordinate seamlessly with our specialist departments when needed. You will never be a number here. You will always be a patient we genuinely care about.

With specialists across Gynaecology, ENT, and Dental under the same roof, our General Medicine team ensures that your care is connected, coordinated, and truly comprehensive, saving you time and giving you peace of mind.

CONSULTING PHYSICIAN

Dr. Viranchi Patel M.D. General Medicine

Dr. Viranchi Patel brings a rare combination of global medical education and deep local roots to every consultation. Trained at B.J. As a proud member of the Patel family that has served Modasa's community since 1987, Dr. Viranchi continues a legacy of honest, affordable, and ethical healthcare, approaching each patient with a holistic perspective that accounts for lifestyle, environment, and personal health goals.
