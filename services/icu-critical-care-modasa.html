ICU & Critical Care Ruchir Hospital, Modasa

At Ruchir Hospital, our Intensive Care Unit provides close, continuous monitoring and specialised medical support for patients whose conditions require a higher level of attention than a standard ward can provide. Our ICU is staffed by qualified physicians and trained medical professionals committed to stabilising critical patients and guiding them safely through the most vulnerable stages of their recovery.

What is Intensive Care?

Intensive care is specialised medical support provided to patients with serious, life-threatening, or rapidly changing conditions that require continuous monitoring of vital signs, advanced medication management, and immediate medical response. ICU care bridges the gap between emergency stabilisation and full recovery ensuring the body is supported through its most critical phase.

What Our ICU Offers

  • Continuous monitoring of vital signs heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen levels, and temperature
  • Advanced medication administration and IV therapy
  • Post-surgical recovery monitoring and support
  • Management of acute medical emergencies cardiac events, severe infections, respiratory distress
  • Close physician oversight with regular patient reviews
  • Coordinated care across General Medicine, ENT, Gynaecology, and Dental departments
  • Family communication and regular updates on patient condition
  • Stabilisation and safe transfer coordination where higher-level care is required

Why Choose Ruchir Hospital's ICU?

Close to Home: For patients and families in Modasa and Arvalli district, Ruchir Hospital's ICU removes the need to travel to larger cities for critical care providing qualified, attentive monitoring right here in your community.

Physician-Led Care: Our ICU is overseen by our qualified medical team including Dr. Viranchi Patel and Dr. Nitesh Prajapati ensuring every critical patient receives consistent, informed, and responsive medical attention.

Coordinated Support: Because all four of our specialty departments operate under one roof, patients in our ICU benefit from immediate access to specialist input across General Medicine, Gynaecology, ENT, and Dental without delays or transfers.

Transparent Communication: We believe families deserve clear, honest updates. Our team ensures that relatives are kept informed throughout a patient's critical care stay.

“Critical care is not just about monitoring numbers it is about watching over a person at their most vulnerable and making sure every decision is made with their full recovery in mind.”
Dr. Viranchi Patel, General Physician, Ruchir Hospital

For over 35 years, Ruchir Hospital has been the healthcare home of Modasa families. Our team of qualified specialists across four departments is committed to honest, patient-centred care every day, for every patient.
