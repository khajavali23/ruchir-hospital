At Ruchir Hospital, our Gynaecology Services are built on a simple belief: every woman deserves to be heard, respected, and given the highest standard of clinical care. From adolescent health concerns to post-menopausal management, our department is a safe and private space where women's health issues are taken seriously and treated with both precision and empathy.

What Do Gynaecology Services Cover?

Gynaecology deals with the health of the female reproductive system, including the uterus, ovaries, fallopian tubes, cervix, and vagina. Gynaecological conditions are extremely common and range from menstrual irregularities and infections to complex surgical conditions requiring expert intervention.

Conditions We Diagnose and Treat

  • Irregular, painful, or heavy menstrual periods
  • Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome (PCOS) and hormonal imbalances
  • Uterine fibroids and ovarian cysts
  • Endometriosis diagnosis and long-term management
  • Pelvic inflammatory disease and vaginal infections
  • Cervical health screenings, Pap smears, and HPV assessment
  • Infertility evaluation and initial fertility guidance
  • Menopausal symptoms — hot flashes, hormonal therapy, and bone health
  • Urinary incontinence and pelvic floor disorders
  • Laparoscopic surgery for gynaecological conditions

Why Choose Ruchir Hospital for Gynaecology?

Privacy and Dignity: Our consultation rooms and procedures are conducted with complete discretion. Women's health deserves a private, comfortable environment, and that is exactly what we provide.

Advanced Surgical Capability: Dr Nidhi Patel performs minimally invasive laparoscopic procedures for conditions including fibroids, cysts, and endometriosis, resulting in less pain, smaller scars, and faster recovery than traditional surgery.

Holistic Approach: Gynaecological health is connected to overall wellbeing. Our team considers your complete health picture, including weight, diet, stress, and other medical conditions, when developing your care plan.

For over 35 years, Ruchir Hospital has been the healthcare home of Modasa families. Our team of qualified specialists across four departments is committed to honest, patient-centred care every day, for every patient.
