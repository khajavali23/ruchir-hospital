Hearing Aid Clinic Ruchir Hospital, Modasa

At Ruchir Hospital's Hearing Aid Clinic, we provide comprehensive audiological assessment and personalised hearing solutions for patients experiencing any degree of hearing loss. Hearing loss is far more common than most people realise and far more treatable. Our clinic combines clinical expertise with compassionate support, helping patients in Modasa and across the Aravalli district reconnect with the sounds that matter most to them.

Understanding Hearing Loss

Hearing loss can develop gradually over years, making it easy to dismiss or overlook. Many patients adapt without realising the extent to which reduced hearing is affecting their relationships, work, and mental wellbeing. Whether your hearing loss is mild, moderate, or severe, early assessment and appropriate intervention can make a profound difference to your quality of life.

Common Causes of Hearing Loss We Assess

  • Age-related hearing decline (Presbycusis)
  • Noise-induced hearing loss from prolonged loud sound exposure
  • Chronic ear infections and fluid in the middle ear
  • Perforated eardrum
  • Earwax blockage causing conductive hearing loss
  • Genetic or congenital hearing loss in children
  • Sudden sensorineural hearing loss requiring urgent assessment

Our Hearing Aid Clinic Services

  • Full audiological evaluation and hearing threshold testing
  • Tympanometry and middle ear function assessment
  • Speech recognition testing
  • Personalised hearing aid selection and fitting
  • Hearing aid follow-up, adjustment, and maintenance
  • Counselling for patients and families on communicating with hearing loss
  • Referral for surgical options where medically indicated

Why Choose Ruchir Hospital's Hearing Aid Clinic?

Qualified Audiologist On-Site: Lakhan Rajput holds a Diploma in Hearing, Language and Speech (D.H.L.S.) and provides professional audiological care with a patient-first approach.

Integrated ENT Support: Our Hearing Aid Clinic works directly with Dr. Jitendra Patil, ENT Surgeon ensuring any underlying medical conditions are properly diagnosed and treated.

Personalised Fitting: We take time to understand your lifestyle and hearing needs to recommend the most suitable hearing aid. Full support is provided during the adjustment period.

At Ruchir Hospital, we believe no one should miss important conversations or feel isolated due to hearing loss. Our goal is to help you reconnect with the world of sound.
