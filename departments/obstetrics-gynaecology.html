Comprehensive Women’s Healthcare – Obstetrics & Gynaecology Services

The Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology at Ruchir Hospital is dedicated to delivering compassionate, expert, and respectful care to women at every stage of life. From adolescence to menopause and every chapter in between, our department is a safe, trusted space where women's health is taken seriously and treated with the highest clinical standard. Our specialists combine advanced surgical training with a deeply human approach, understanding that every woman's health journey is personal. Whether you are planning a family, navigating a pregnancy, or seeking treatment for a gynaecological condition, you will find experienced hands and a reassuring voice at Ruchir Hospital.

Our Obstetrics and Gynaecology department provides expert care for routine and complex women's health needs. From prenatal monitoring and maternity care to advanced laparoscopic procedures and hormonal health management, we offer personalised treatment plans designed around every patient's unique needs.

  • Normal and high-risk pregnancy monitoring and care.
  • Prenatal scans, diagnostics, and foetal assessments.
  • Safe delivery and post-natal care.
  • Laparoscopic and minimally invasive gynaecological surgeries.
  • Menstrual irregularities, PCOS, and hormonal disorders.
  • Uterine fibroids, ovarian cysts, and endometriosis.
  • Infertility evaluation and assisted conception guidance.
  • Cervical and gynaecological cancer screening.
  • Menopausal health management and counselling.
  • Family planning advice and contraception consultations.

Why Choose Ruchir Hospital for Women's Health?

At Ruchir Hospital, we believe every woman deserves to feel heard, respected, and cared for, not rushed through a consultation. Our Obstetrics and Gynaecology department is designed with privacy, comfort, and dignity in mind. From your first prenatal visit to post-surgical recovery, we are with you at every step. Our in-house prenatal scanning and diagnostic facility means you get immediate results without being sent to another clinic. Combined with our General Medicine and other departments, we provide truly holistic women's healthcare under one trusted roof.

OBSTETRICS & GYNAECOLOGY SPECIALIST

Dr Nidhi Patel M.S. Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Dr Nidhi Patel is one of Gujarat's accomplished specialists in both Fetal Medicine and advanced Laparoscopic Surgery. With her M.S. from AMC-MET Medical College, Maninagar, and further specialisation in Obstetrics and Gynaecology from NHL Municipal Medical College, Ahmedabad, Dr Nidhi combines precise surgical skill with genuine empathy for every patient in her care. Her expertise in foetal medicine ensures that complex pregnancies are managed with clinical accuracy and personal sensitivity, giving expectant mothers in Aravalli district access to the kind of specialised care usually found only in larger cities.
