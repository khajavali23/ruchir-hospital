Advanced Ear, Nose & Throat Care – Expert Diagnosis & Treatment

The Department of Ear, Nose, and Throat at Ruchir Hospital addresses a wide spectrum of conditions that affect your sensory experience, your ability to breathe freely, and your quality of daily life. From a child's recurring ear infection to complex sinus disorders in adults, our ENT department delivers precise diagnosis and effective treatment with a calm, expert approach. Our team understands that ENT conditions, however they may seem on the surface, can significantly disrupt sleep, communication, and overall wellbeing. We are committed to restoring your comfort quickly, safely, and with lasting results.

Dr. Nidhi Patil combines thorough Hospitalsal expertise with a warm, reassuring manner that puts even the most apprehensive patients at ease. From routine check-ups to complex gynecological conditions and antenatal care, she ensures that every woman receives the highest standard of personalised care throughout every stage of her life.

Why Choose Ruchir Hospital for ENT Care?

ENT conditions rarely exist in isolation. A persistent headache may stem from a sinus issue. Hearing loss can affect a child's development and learning. At Ruchir Hospital, our ENT specialist works closely with the General Medicine department to ensure that interconnected conditions are treated together, not in isolation. We also offer a dedicated Hearing Aid Clinic for patients experiencing hearing loss, providing audiological testing and professional guidance on the most suitable hearing solutions right here in Modasa, without the need to travel to larger cities.
